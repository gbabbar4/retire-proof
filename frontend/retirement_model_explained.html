Estimated retirement survival probability (0-100%). The neural network predicts the likelihood of retirement funds lasting through the modeled planning horizon, up to age 90.

In your own words

Retire-Proof uses a fully connected feedforward neural network built from scratch using NumPy. The model learns from simulated retirement scenarios and estimates the probability of retirement funds lasting through the planning horizon. It uses seven financial inputs, ReLU activation in hidden layers, and a sigmoid output to produce a probability between 0 and 1.

RESEARCH WITH RESPONSIBILITY

What it can - and can't - say.

Every model card should be as clear about uncertainty as it is about performance.

Performance ≠ certainty

A low validation loss doesn't mean every individual's retirement outcome can be predicted accurately. Inspect errors across different cases.

Data shapes the answer

Results depend on the training data, feature quality, preprocessing, and whether new users resemble training examples.

Financial forecasts are uncertain

Market returns, inflation, life events, and policy changes can alter retirement outcomes. Use predictions as scenarios, not guarantees.
